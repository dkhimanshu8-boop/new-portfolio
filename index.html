SEO that runs on agents

Most of the repeatable work in SEO can be handed to an agent with the right tools. I build those pipelines with Claude Code, MCP servers and Python, and keep the judgement calls for myself.

  • Automated news publishing

    An end-to-end publishing setup for a media agency: sourcing, drafting, on-page optimisation and scheduling, with a human approval step before anything goes live.

  • GEO and AI-visibility monitoring

    Tracking how brands appear inside AI answers, not just blue links — and turning the gaps into content and schema changes that move both.

  • Audit tooling

    Screaming Frog and Semrush exports in, a prioritised client-ready workbook out: one sheet per issue, evidence columns only, one crisp fix per row.

  • Ahrefs and Search Console over MCP

    Live rank, backlink and query data pulled straight into the analysis, so reports are built from the numbers rather than screenshots of them.
